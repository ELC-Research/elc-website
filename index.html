Ecological Languaging Competencies

A research hub for reimagining language education through embodied, distributed, and adaptive practice.

About

The ELC Research Hub brings together scholars, teachers, and educators working at the intersection of applied linguistics, ecological theory, and classroom practice. We develop frameworks, tools, and resources that treat language learning as wayfinding — an embodied, multimodal journey through social and material environments.

Our work is grounded in the conviction that language cannot be separated from the body, the social world, or the material contexts in which it unfolds. Competence is not possession; it is coordination.

Our Framework

The Ecological Languaging Competencies (ELC) framework draws on social theory, multimodal interaction, and dynamic systems thinking to reconceptualise what it means to know and use a language.

MEC

Multimodal Embodied Competence — languaging as situated action involving bodily, spatial, and digital resources.

4T Lenses

Text, Task, Topic, and Trajectory — a heuristic for designing and evaluating language learning experiences.

Wayfinding

Education as navigation through complex semiotic landscapes, rather than linear progression toward fixed targets.

Assessment

Rubric design and feedback systems that capture growth in coordination, not merely correctness.

Collaborators

ELC Research is a collaborative network spanning Hong Kong, Alberta, Sydney, and beyond. We work with teachers, doctoral researchers, and institutional partners who share a commitment to ecological approaches in language education.

Current projects include AI-enabled learning design, translanguaging pedagogies, and genre-based assessment innovation.
